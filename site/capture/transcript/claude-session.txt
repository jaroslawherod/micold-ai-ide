### step
> Why does stale() sort vehicles that never reported before the ones that went quiet?

I read src/telemetry.rs. It is deliberate, and docs/operations.md says so:

  A vehicle that was never heard from is a worse problem than one that went quiet
  an hour ago.

`last_seen` is `Option<u64>`, and `sort_by_key` puts `None` before every `Some`, so the
ordering falls out of the type rather than out of a comparison written by hand.

> 
### step
> The fix branch changes > to >=. Is that right?

Yes, and it is the smaller of the two defensible answers.

  fix/telemetry-drift  src/telemetry.rs

  - .filter(|v| v.last_seen.is_none_or(|seen| now.saturating_sub(seen) > window))
  + .filter(|v| v.last_seen.is_none_or(|seen| now.saturating_sub(seen) >= window))

A vehicle exactly at the window edge was reported as healthy, so a fleet polled on the
same period as the window never went stale at all. The alternative -- widening the
window -- hides the same bug behind a number.
