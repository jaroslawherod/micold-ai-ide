# Bug Report: the overflow menu opens 13dp inside the app bar, over the trigger it came from — and the project switcher, its unmerged twin, does the same at a different row height

**Type**: Implementation drift, over a spec gap (nothing states where a menu panel sits relative to
the bar it hangs from), over duplicated construction (three modules build the same item row).
**Severity**: High — the application's primary menu, on every screen, drawn across the bar it
belongs to; the switcher panel beside it has the same fault and disagrees with it about row height.
**Feature**: `018-material3-visual-system` (§7.1 top app bar, §7.5 menus; FR-025, FR-029). The
offending constant belongs to `005-worktree-session-terminal`, which is where it was written.
**Reported**: 2026-08-07
**Status**: Fixed
**Patched**: 2026-08-07 — spec artifacts updated: FR-029a, FR-029b, US4 acceptance scenario 11 and
SC-008c (spec.md); §7.1's divider and bottom-edge rows and §7.5's abutting-items, panel-top-edge and
one-row-built-once entries (contracts/design-tokens.md); the restated-constant and
single-component-scope risk rows (plan.md); T059 reopened and T100–T110 added (tasks.md).
**Fixed**: 2026-08-07 on `fix/meanu-layout-is-broken` (T100–T109, T111), behind failing tests.
**Branch**: `fix/meanu-layout-is-broken`

## Description

Opening the toolbar's overflow (⋮) menu draws the panel **on top of the app bar**: the panel's top
edge lands 13dp above the bar's lower boundary, clipping the bottom of the ⋮ button the user just
pressed and covering the bar's divider across the panel's whole width.

The panel is positioned by `Anchor::TopEnd { top: TOP_OFFSET, .. }`, and
`crates/micold-client/src/ui/material/menu.rs:60-61` reads:

```rust
/// Vertical offset so the panel clears the toolbar (approx. toolbar height).
const TOP_OFFSET: f32 = 52.0;
```

"Approx. toolbar height" was written in feature 005, when the bar was content-sized. §7.1 later
fixed the bar at **64dp**, and `toolbar.rs` adds a **1dp** divider beneath it. Nothing connects the
two numbers, so the offset is 13dp short and has been since the bar was pinned.

Three further §7.5 figures are unapplied in the same panel, which is why the rows also read loose
and the icons small — see Evidence. `4cd33b6` (T098) applied §7.5's 48dp item height, which grew the
panel from 204dp to 264dp and made all of this considerably more visible; it did not cause the
overlap, which is the same 13dp before and after.

So the defect is in two places, and the row height that was fixed in one of them was not fixed in
the other: two panels on the same `menu_panel` surface, hanging from the same bar, one with 48dp
rows and one with 36dp.

## Artifact Traceability

### spec.md

- **Affected user story**: US4 (correct anatomy — "menus … match Material's menu anatomy").
- **Affected requirements**: **FR-029** (menu anatomy, "item density"), **FR-025** (the app bar's
  64dp) — met individually, and in conflict once composed.
- **Gap identified**: no requirement states **where a menu panel sits relative to the component it
  hangs from**. FR-008 (feature 017) gives the overlay layer positioning and z-order; §7.5 gives the
  panel its internal anatomy; nothing owns the join. FR-030a is the precedent — it exists because a
  height above its content obliges the contract to say where the content goes. The same argument
  applies one level out: a panel anchored *below* something obliges the contract to say below
  **what**, or the anchor becomes a hand-tuned duplicate of a number that lives elsewhere.
- **Affected success criteria**: SC-008 (constants match the contract) is green and blind here;
  SC-008a reads drawn ink of one component; SC-008b reads one component's laid-out size. None of the
  three reads two components against each other.

### contracts/design-tokens.md

- **§7.1** — "Height 64". Correct and applied.
- **§7.5** — the menu table. One of its five spatial figures is applied. The table needs the same
  treatment §7.6 got under BUG-001: a row stating the panel's placement relative to the app bar, so
  the offset stops being folklore in a comment.

### plan.md

- The **component-boundary** section (`material` owns appearance, `cdk::overlay` owns placement).
  `menu.rs`'s own module doc states "Floating is not this module's job … what is left here is
  appearance", and then declares `TOP_OFFSET`, which is placement. That is the boundary the fix has
  to restore, not just the number.

### tasks.md

- **False completion — T059**: "Apply menu anatomy in `menu.rs` — `surface_container`, elevation 2,
  `extra_small` corner, 48dp items (FR-029)", marked `[X]`. The 48dp items did not land until T098
  five phases later, and three of §7.5's remaining figures are still unapplied today. The task was
  closed on the colour and shape half of §7.5 alone.
- **Incomplete completion — T098** (`4cd33b6`): applied §7.5's item height and correctly moved
  `menu_panel_size` onto the token, and stopped at the one figure the task named. The panel it made
  60dp taller sits under an offset nothing re-derived.
- **Not a false completion — T088/T092** (BUG-001/BUG-002's sweeps): both swept for a *container*
  overwriting or failing to place its own content. `TOP_OFFSET` is neither shape, so a correct sweep
  could not have found it.
- **Missing tasks**: see Recommended Fix — derive the offset, apply §7.5's remaining figures, cover

### Cross-feature

## Root Cause Analysis

**The constant is a copy of another component's dimension, taken by eye, in the module that is
documented as not owning placement.** `TOP_OFFSET = 52.0` was approximately right for a
content-sized bar in feature 005. Feature 018 pinned the bar at 64dp and added a divider; the copy
did not move, because nothing links a copy to its original. `4cd33b6` cured precisely this shape one
constant over — `menu_panel_size` rebuilt an item's height from padding plus a line box, and now
reads `density::MENU_ITEM_BASE`, with the commit message noting that the arithmetic "would have gone
wrong the moment the height landed". The same argument was available for the constant on line 61 and
was not made.

**And it is in two files because the item row was never unified.** Feature 017 merged five overlay
implementations into one — but what it merged is **positioning, backdrop, dismissal and z-order**
(`cdk::overlay`), and `one_overlay_implementation.rs` closes exactly that list. The **panel content**
was left alone, and three modules still build the same thing by hand:

| | panel surface | row height | row padding | gap | leading icon | ripple |
|-|-|-:|-:|-:|-:|-|
| `menu.rs` | `menu_panel` | `Fixed(48)` | `[0, 8]` | 4 | 14 (`Action.size()`) | explicit |
| `typeahead.rs` | `menu_panel` | `Fixed(48)` | `[0, 8]` | 0 | — | explicit |
| `project_switcher.rs` | `menu_panel` | *(unset → 36)* | `8` | 4 | 12 (`Label.size()`) | explicit |
| §7.5 | — | 48 | `_, 12` | — | 24 | — |

Each is a `column![]` of `Ripple::new(button(row![icon, label]).style(text_button(r)), …,
shape::FULL)` — the same widget, the same style, the same ripple, written out three times. Nothing
names "a menu item" as a component, so `MenuItem` is a *data* struct one of the three happens to
own, not a thing that can be built once and reused. That is why T098's fix reached one of the three
call sites and left the switcher 12dp shorter than the menu it sits beside, and why `TOP_OFFSET` had
to be got right twice and was got wrong twice.

Constitution Principle VIII asks for reusable primitives, and the trigger halves obey it —
`MenuTrigger` and `ProjectSwitcherTrigger` are separate because they genuinely differ (an icon
versus an icon-and-label). The panel halves do not differ: a switcher row is a menu item with a
leading marker, a trailing count and a right-press. Those are three fields on `MenuItem`, not a
second implementation.

**Why every gate was green.** Four gates cover this area and each is blind to it by construction:

| Gate | Reads | Why it cannot see this |
|------|-------|------------------------|
| `tokens_anatomy` (SC-008) | constant *values* | 64, 48, 8, 12, 24 are all correct. It never asks whether anything reads them. |
| `anatomy_size` (SC-008b) | one component's laid-out node, under two limits | The menu item **is** 48dp. Components are measured in isolation, never composed. |
| `content_placement` (SC-008a) | one component's drawn ink | Answers where content sits *within* a component. Blind to where the component sits. |
| `containment` (BUG-001) | child vs. its own parent | The panel is well inside its overlay parent. Overlapping a *sibling* is not an escape. |
| `layout_snapshot` (FR-003) | whole composed screens | The only gate that could have caught it — and the state is not covered. It also records rather than asserts, so had it been covered from the start it would have adopted the 13dp as the baseline, exactly as it did for BUG-002. |

The missing assertion class is **relative position between two independent components**. Every gate
this feature built reads one component, or a parent/child pair, or a byte-compare of a recorded
whole. Nothing says "the panel that opens below the app bar begins below the app bar".

**And the constants nobody reads.** BUG-002's lesson — "a constant asserted by `tokens_anatomy` and
referenced by no component is not applied" — was drawn twice (BUG-002, then T097/T098 by hand during
convergence) and never turned into a check. Four such constants are sitting in `anatomy::menu` right
now, which is how §7.5's padding, item padding and icon size all reached the shipped panel unapplied
while their gate stayed green.

## Recommended Fix

Ordered so the guards land before the changes, per Principle I and BUG-002's resolution.

1. **Cover the state first** (feature 019 FR-016). One entry in `tests/support/covered_states.rs`:
   `toolbar-overflow-menu-open`, with anchors for the app bar, the ⋮ trigger and the panel — and a
   second, `project-switcher-open`, since that panel has the same fault and is equally uncovered.
   One edit, one place. Regenerating the fixture then records the defect — that is intended, and is
   what makes step 2's diff the proof.
2. **Add the assertion the gates lack.** A new gate (alongside `containment`, sharing the same
   record cache) asserting, over every covered state: an anchored overlay panel does not intersect
   the app bar's band, and lies wholly within the window. It must **assert**, not record — the whole
   point is that a snapshot regenerates a defect into its own baseline.
3. **Derive the offset, do not tune it.** Either `TOP_OFFSET = anatomy::app_bar::HEIGHT +
   app_bar::DIVIDER`, or — better, and what the module doc asks for — an `Anchor::BelowAppBar`
   variant in `cdk::overlay`, so `menu.rs` stops stating another component's height at all and
   placement lives where the boundary says it does.
4. **Apply the rest of §7.5** — `menu::VERTICAL_PADDING` (8) on the panel, `menu::ITEM_PADDING` (12)
   horizontally in an item, `menu::ITEM_ICON` (24) for the leading glyph, and drop the 4dp
   inter-item gap the contract does not have. `menu_panel_size` must move with all of them; it is
   now correct, and each of these silently invalidates it. Extend `anatomy_size` with entries that
   read those three constants, so they stop being asserted only by `tokens_anatomy`.
5. **Unify the item row.** Promote the column `menu.rs` builds to the one component every menu-like
   panel uses — `menu.rs` (already there), `typeahead.rs`, and `project_switcher.rs`, whose rows are
   `MenuItem` plus a leading marker, a trailing count and an `on_right_press`. Delete
   `project_switcher.rs`'s duplicate `TOP_OFFSET` with it. Then close the class the way feature 017
   closed its own: a ratchet asserting that `button(...).style(text_button(...))` inside a
   `menu_panel` is built in exactly one place, with a closed list of exceptions. Without that, the
   fourth copy arrives the way the third did. **This is what makes the rest of the fixes stick** —
   every item above has to be applied once instead of three times, and the next §7.5 figure cannot
   reach two panels out of three.
6. **Close the class, not the instance.** A structural scan — every constant in
   `tokens/anatomy.rs` must be referenced by at least one component, not only by
   `tokens_anatomy.rs`. That single check would have caught BUG-002, T097, T098 and items 4 above,
   before any of them shipped. It is the generalisable outcome of this bug.
7. **Reopen T059** with the four unapplied figures named, and record the deviation or the fix per
   FR-029, the way T097/T098 were framed.
8. **Confirm by eye** per quickstart §B4, with the overflow menu and the switcher open in turn — the
   ⋮ must remain fully visible, both panels must begin below the bar's divider, and their rows must
   be the same height.

Then: `/speckit.bugfix.patch` to update the spec artifacts, `/speckit.bugfix.verify` for
consistency, `/speckit.implement` for the code fix.

## Resolution

**The guards first.** `tests/gates/panel_placement.rs` (T101) asserts what no gate here could ask —
a relationship between two independent components — and was confirmed red on **17 panels across 16
covered states**, not the two this bug was reported about: the closed overflow menu is laid out in
every state, so the 13dp was everywhere. `src/ui/material/menu_anatomy.rs` (T103) took §7.5's four
unapplied figures one at a time and confirmed each red: a 14dp glyph against 24, an 8dp inset
against 12, 4dp of panel padding against 8, and 4dp between items where the contract has nothing.

That second file found a defect in the *harness* on its way: the in-crate renderer loaded the two
Roboto faces and not the icon face, so a 14dp glyph resolved through the host's fallback and
measured 8.4dp. Feature 019's out-of-crate harness had the identical gap and closed it; every
in-crate test that has ever read an icon's box was reading whatever font the machine offered.

**The fix.** `anatomy::app_bar::BOTTOM_EDGE` (T102) is the number both panels now read; neither
states one. `MenuItem` gained the five fields a switcher row needs and
`project_switcher::row_column` became a `map` into `MenuItem`s followed by `menu::item_column`
(T104) — its hand-built `button`/`text_button`/`Ripple`/`mouse_area` stack is gone, and with it the
second copy of `TOP_OFFSET`. §7.5 then applied once and reached both panels (T105).

**Measured, from the regenerated fixture (T109):**

| | before | after |
|-|-|-|
| overflow panel | `1032, 52, 240 × 264` | `1032, 65, 240 × 256` |
| switcher panel | `1012, 52, 260 × 84` | `1012, 65, 260 × 112` |
| menu item | 48dp tall, 8dp ends, 14dp glyph, 4dp gaps | 48dp tall, 12dp ends, 24dp glyph, abutting |
| switcher row | **36dp** tall, 8dp ends, 12dp glyph | the same row, byte for byte |

Both panels' top edge is now exactly the bar's bottom edge. The trigger is unobstructed.

**Two things fell out of it.** The item's label now fills the width its 12dp ends leave it, which
made both of `layout_snapshot.rs`'s `SCHEME_DEPENDENT` exemptions go stale — the theme-mode row's
width no longer depends on the string inside it — so that list is empty and the geometry is
scheme-independent without exception. And the sweep (T107) found the worktree and session context
menus anchored at a literal `Point::new(24.0, 96.0)` twice with **no clamping at all**, beside a
project menu that clamps; both now clamp, and the point is stated once. It had not bitten yet and
was about to, a four-item menu having grown from 152dp to 200dp when items became 48dp.

**What is not covered.** T110's look at the running application was not done — see its note. The
confirmation needs the application *driven*, and the only route from a shell here acts on the
owner's live desktop; the attempt raised a Remote Desktop consent dialog over their work and was
abandoned rather than pushed through. Every geometric claim is held by T100/T101/T103. What none of
them reads is drawn pixels, and `content_placement` was not extended to the menu.

**Caught inside the fix (T111).** §7.5's item-icon row states a size *and* a tone, and the first
pass applied the size only — a figure in the table reaching no component, which is this bug's own
shape repeated inside its own fix. The leading glyph is now `on_surface_variant` through a new
`IconSurface::MenuItem`, asserted before it was changed. It gets its own context rather than reusing
`Badge`, which resolves to the same role today for different reasons.

**Left open, deliberately.** The generalisable check — *every constant in `tokens/anatomy.rs` is
referenced by at least one component, not only by `tokens_anatomy.rs`* — is written up in the
Recommended Fix and not built. It would have caught BUG-002, T097, T098 and three of this bug's four
figures before any of them shipped, and it is the single highest-value thing left here. It is not
free: `anatomy::menu::DIVIDER` has no consumer today because no menu in the application groups its
items, so the check lands with an exemption on its first day, and an exemption written at the same
moment as the rule it exempts is worth arguing about in its own diff rather than in this one.
