# Bug Report: the sidebar is two tones — its resize handle's grab zone is painted `surface`, the panel `surface_container`

**Type**: Implementation drift. §4 puts the sidebar panel at elevation `CARD`, and the panel honours
it. The resize handle on the panel's right edge paints its grab zone in `roles.surface` — elevation 0
— under a comment saying it is "painted in the panel's own surface colour". It was, before the panel
was raised; the handle was not moved with it.

**Severity**: Low — cosmetic, no loss of function. But it is visible on every frame with a project
open and the sidebar expanded, in both schemes, and it is exactly the kind of false edge US1 exists to
remove: a band of window-background tone that reads as a gap between the panel and its border.

**Feature**: `018-material3-visual-system` (§4 elevation levels; FR-015, FR-016; US1).

**Reported**: 2026-09-13

**Status**: Fixed

**Patched**: 2026-09-13

## Description

Reported from a screenshot of the running client (dark scheme, 2× scale). Sampling it:

| x (device px) | Colour | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 197 | `#1C1B1E` (28,27,30) | the sidebar panel — `surface_container`, elevation `CARD` |
| 198 – 207 | `#141316` (20,19,22) | the handle's grab zone — `surface` |
| 208 – 209 | `#49454E` (73,69,78) | the handle's rule — the separator |
| 210 – | `#141316` | the main area — `background` |

The 10px band is `ResizeHandle::WIDTH − RULE` = 5dp at 2×. So the panel reads as ending 5dp short of
its own border, with a strip of the main area's tone inside it. The app bar above is also `surface`,
so the band visually joins the bar and the main area rather than the panel — "two colour background".

## Artifact Traceability

### spec.md

- **Affected user story**: US1 (real depth), acceptance scenario 1 — the window background and the
  sidebar surface render at distinct tonal levels. They do, but the sidebar is *not one* level: its
  outer 5dp is drawn at the background's.
- **Affected requirements**: FR-015 (a surface's depth comes from its named elevation level), FR-016
  (the dark scheme relies on the tonal shift — which is why the band is most visible there).
- **Gap identified**: none in the spec. The spec is right; the handle does not follow it.

### plan.md

- **Affected sections**: the style layer's elevation mapping (`style::elevated`,
  `style::sidebar_surface`) and the `ResizeHandle` component.
- **Impact**: none. No design decision changes.

### tasks.md

- **Affected tasks**: T001 (`style_elevation.rs` — asserts each elevated *style function* returns its
  level's tone). The handle is not a style function: it paints a raw quad in `draw`, so T001's gate
  cannot see it.
- **False completions**: none. T001 did what it said; the handle's fill was never in its scope.
- **Missing tasks**: a test that the handle's grab zone and the sidebar surface resolve to the same
  colour in both schemes, and the fix that makes it pass — with one source for the tone so the two
  cannot drift again.

## Root Cause Analysis

`crates/micold-client/src/ui/material/resize_handle.rs` `draw` fills the grab zone with
`style::color(self.roles.surface)`, a restatement of "the panel's colour" as a literal role.
`style::sidebar_surface` resolves the panel through `elevated(r, elevation::CARD, …)`, i.e.
`r.elevation_surface(CARD)` = `surface_container`. Two statements of one fact, and when the panel's
level moved to `CARD` only one of them moved. Nothing asserts they agree, because every existing
elevation gate reads container styles and the handle draws a quad.

## Recommended Fix

1. Run `/speckit.bugfix.patch` to add the missing tasks to `tasks.md`.
2. Give the sidebar's fill one source in the style layer and have both `sidebar_surface` and the
   handle's grab zone read it; add a test asserting the handle's fill equals the sidebar surface's
   background in both schemes.
3. Confirm on a real frame that the panel is one tone up to the rule.
