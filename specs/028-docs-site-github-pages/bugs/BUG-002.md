# Bug Report: The site's header does not read as the application's top app bar

**Type**: Implementation drift: the title is pinned to the top of the bar and the icons are
mdBook's. There is also a spec gap: the contract never says which way the title is aligned.
**Severity**: Medium. FR-029a names the header as one of the two treatments that carry the
resemblance to the application. It is the first thing on every page and the part that looks least
like the application. Nothing is unreadable or unreachable.
**Feature**: 028-docs-site-github-pages (User Story 1 scenario 5; FR-029, FR-029a, FR-031)
**Reported**: 2026-09-16
**Status**: Patched
**Patched**: 2026-09-16

## Description

The quickstart Part B pass (B3) compared the site's header with the app bar in the application
screenshot directly below it on the home page: [evidence/quickstart-b.md](../evidence/quickstart-b.md),
finding 2. See [the site's header](../evidence/quickstart-b3-site-header.png) and [the app bar in
the screenshot](../evidence/quickstart-b3-app-bar.png). There are three differences, in both
schemes and at phone width:

1. **The title sits against the top edge of the bar.** Measured at 1366×768, `#mdbook-menu-bar` is
   65 px tall and `.menu-title` is a 64 px box with `line-height: 28px`. The text therefore fills
   the top 28 px, while the controls beside it are centred at 32 px. The application centres its
   title vertically with its actions.
2. **The title is centred horizontally** (`text-align: center` across the space between the button
   groups). The application's app bar starts its title at the leading edge.
3. **The header's icons are not the application's.** All five (menu, theme, search, repository,
   edit) are mdBook's inline Font Awesome SVGs, drawn by `{{fa …}}` in `site/theme/index.hbs`
   (lines 172, 175, 184, 199, 208), and so are the previous/next chevrons. The site ships Material
   Symbols Outlined (`site/theme/css/site.css` `@font-face`, lines 53–64) but draws nothing in its
   own chrome with it.

What does match: the surface-container shade, no outline, Roboto, and the elevation change on
scroll.

## Artifact Traceability

### spec.md

- **Affected user story**: User Story 1, scenario 5: a visitor looking at a screenshot embedded in
  a page sees one design language, not two.
- **Affected requirements**:
  - **FR-029**: "A reader who moves between a screenshot and the page around it MUST NOT see two
    different design languages."
  - **FR-029a**: "the header is the application's top app bar".
  - **FR-031**: "The site MUST use the typeface and icon set the application ships — the same
    Roboto and the same Material Symbols files".
- **Gap identified**: FR-029a names the app bar, but nothing says what makes a header *be* it
  beyond colour and type. The title's alignment and the icons are left to the implementation.

### contracts/theme-variables.md

- **Affected**: *What the site is allowed to do with them*: "the header is the application's top
  app bar: its surface role, its elevation, its title type".
- **Impact**: the contract lists three properties and the implementation meets exactly those three.
  Title placement (vertically centred, start-aligned) and the icon set are missing, so a header can
  satisfy the contract and still not look like the app bar.

### plan.md

- **Affected sections**: *Project Structure*, `site/theme/index.hbs`, "app-bar header, table of
  contents, version, source link".
- **Impact**: the header is described only as a template. The plan should say that the template's
  icons are drawn from the application's icon font, not mdBook's helper.

### research.md

- **Affected sections**: §4 *Fonts, icons, and the monospace exception*. It decides to serve
  `MaterialSymbolsOutlined.ttf` but not where the site uses it.
- **Impact**: add that every icon in the site's chrome comes from that font, and name each glyph
  that replaces a Font Awesome icon.

### tasks.md

- **Affected tasks**:
  - **T032**: implement the app-bar header in `site/theme/index.hbs` and `site/theme/css/site.css`
    with the top-app-bar surface role, elevation and title type.
  - **T016**: `site.css` furniture, including the `@font-face` for Material Symbols.
- **False completions**: **T032**, in part. The title type is applied, but applying it
  (`line-height`) moved the title out of the bar's vertical centre, which is a regression T032
  introduced. The icons were never switched.
- **Missing tasks**:
  - A rendered-page check in `site/checks/page-checks.mjs`: the title's text box is vertically
    centred in the bar within a pixel or two, starts at the leading edge after the navigation
    control, and no `svg` from mdBook's `fa` helper remains in `#mdbook-menu-bar` or
    `.nav-chapters`. It should be seen red on `main`.
  - Fix the title: centre its line box in the bar without losing the title-large type, and align it
    to the start.
  - Replace every `{{fa …}}` in the site's chrome with the matching Material Symbols glyph, keeping
    each control's accessible name.

## Root Cause Analysis

mdBook centres `.menu-title` vertically by setting its `line-height` to the bar's height. T032
replaced that `line-height` with the title-large token to get the application's title type, and
nothing checked where the text then sat. The header was implemented to the contract's three named
properties. Horizontal alignment and the icons were never named anywhere, so mdBook's defaults
stayed, and FR-031's icon clause was read as "ship the font" rather than "draw with it". The
automated checks look at contrast, reachability and off-origin requests, none of which can see
either difference; only the Part B judgement could.
